How to try it

  • Start on the kiosk and answer as a buyer. Try "Safety" or "Performance": the match changes, and it can recommend a rival or the Arena Victoris.
  • Each screen scales to fit your window. The kiosk clears itself after 60 seconds untouched, for privacy.

What is real and what is proposed

  • Prices, efficiency and ratings are sourced (Aug–Oct 2026); tap Sources on the kiosk.
  • The Higher Ground Promise, Service Pledge and Weekend Ownership are programme proposals, labelled "Proposed".
  • Sessions on the tablet are samples. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
