# Images needed

No press photography is used. Every car image is a placeholder silhouette that names the shot. To replace one, save a WebP as `public/cars/<id>.webp` and rebuild: the build lists what is in that folder, so nothing is probed over the network.

Use images you have the right to use (your own photographs, or assets licensed for the competition). Do not copy Maruti Suzuki, NEXA, Arena or rival brand imagery.

All shots: 2400 × 1600, landscape, three-quarter front from the driver's side, showroom or soft daylight, plain background, no people, no number plates, no dealer branding.
